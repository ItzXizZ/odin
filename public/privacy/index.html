Privacy Policy

Odin (odinwrite.com) · Last updated June 27, 2026

Odin is an AI writing workspace. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account info — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google.
  • Your content — Documents, notes, uploads, and workspace data you create in Odin.
  • Usage data — Basic logs needed to run the service (e.g. errors, request timing).

How we use it

We use your information to authenticate you, save your workspace, and provide AI features (writing, research, grading). We do not sell your personal data.

Third-party services

  • Google — Sign-in only. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — Account auth and cloud storage for your workspace.
  • Anthropic — AI processing when you use writing, exploration, or grading features. Content you send is processed to generate responses.

Data storage & security

Your workspace is stored in your account on Supabase when cloud sync is enabled. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can stop using Odin at any time. To delete your account and data, contact us at the email below.
